What Pace never does

Each limit is enforced in code, not left to the model, and the code is open. Here is what each one means and where to check it.

Answer anyone but you

Pace runs with your login, so it answers only the Slack member you set as its owner. Anyone else who mentions it gets a note only they can see. Teammates each run their own Pace.

Check it: How it works

Answer in private channels or DMs

The Slack app asks for no private-channel or DM scopes, and Pace answers only in public channels it has been invited to. When it cannot tell whether a channel is public, it does not answer. Its answers leave out your tasks from private channels and DMs, too.

Check it: the Slack app's scopes

Send messages for you

It replies only in the thread you asked in. Replies it drafts for your tasks are shown to you to copy, and nothing is posted until you post it. Draft PRs and Jira issues or comments are written only through connections whose Write you turn on in Settings > Tools; Write starts off.

Check it: Your tasks

Change your files

On your Mac, the CLI gets read-only access to one folder you choose and never your credential folders, such as ~/.ssh or your AI tools' logins. In the built-in sandbox, each answer runs in a Linux VM that reaches only the domains you allow and is thrown away afterwards.

Check it: Reasoner sandbox

Hand your credentials to the model

Slack and hub tokens never reach the CLI, and Pace takes its own secrets out of every answer. With connections on, SSH keys and cluster, GitHub and Jira tokens stay in a separate broker, which the model asks for read-only lookups and, where you turn on Write, draft PRs and Jira issues or comments.

Check it: Tools

Open a public endpoint or phone home

It connects out to Slack over Socket Mode, so nothing on your Mac accepts connections from the internet: only your own Macs reach it, on your own network: ones with the same Slack app once you turn that on, or a new one while Add a Mac waits for it. There is no telemetry; everything it contacts is listed below.

Check it: How it works

Charge you

Pacenote is free and open source. Answers run through the official CLI on the Claude or ChatGPT plan you already pay for, and count toward its usage limits.

Check it: the Apache License 2.0

What stays on your Mac

Settings, run history, your tasks and recaps live in ~/.pacenote, a folder only you can read. Run history is kept for 30 days unless you change it. With Settings > Macs on, your tasks and recaps are kept on your other Mac too. Uninstalling keeps the folder; delete it to remove them.

This website

pacenote.dev counts its visits with Google Analytics: the pages you view, the site that sent you here, whether you copy the install command, and your rough location and device, kept in a cookie Google sets. It helps tell what people read. The app has nothing of it and sends nothing to Google.

Report a security issue

Report it privately through GitHub's vulnerability reporting for the repository. The report stays between you and the maintainer until an advisory is published, so please keep the details out of public issues.
