Join your team's hub

A teammate shared their team's Pacenote hub with you. Open it in Pacenote, then pair your Mac with your own Slack account.

Open in Pacenote

The hub:

This link names no hub. Ask your teammate to copy it again, or enter the hub's address in Pacenote under Settings > Messengers > Slack.

Pair in three steps

  1. Choose Open in Pacenote. Settings > Messengers > Slack opens with the hub filled in, or setup's Slack step while you set Pacenote up. Nothing changes until you connect.
  2. Choose Connect, then mention @Pace with the code Pacenote shows, in a channel Pace is in.
  3. Confirm in Pacenote that the Slack account is yours. Your mentions then come to your Mac, and Pace answers only you.

No Pacenote yet?

Install it with Homebrew on an Apple silicon Mac where Claude Code or Codex is signed in, then open this link again: setup's Slack step takes the hub from it.

brew tap ashon/tap brew trust ashon/tap brew install --cask pacenote

What you need and how setup goes are on Install.

The hub's address stays with you

It is after the # in this link, which your browser never sends: pacenote.dev and link previews never see it, and this page counts nothing. A hub on your company's network answers only where your Mac can reach that network.
