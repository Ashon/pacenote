What's new

Ask in Slack.
Pick it up in your terminal.

Pacenote is a free Mac app. Its assistant, Pace, answers you in Slack when you mention it, with your own Claude Code or Codex login, and hands each task, with its history, to the terminal session that does the work.

brew install --cask ashon/tap/pacenote
How it works

Free and open source. Needs an Apple silicon Mac, your Claude or ChatGPT plan and a Slack workspace where you can create an app. No telemetry.

Every tool reports back to Pacenote

Asks come in from Slack threads, work happens in Claude Code, Codex or opencode, and each one tells Pacenote how it ended. It all stays as one history, on your Mac or on both of your Macs, so the next task starts with what came before, in either tool, on either Mac.

Pace answers in the thread

Mention @Pace in a Slack thread. It reads the thread and its files, works the question out with the claude or codex CLI you are signed in to, and answers right there. Every step it took is kept.

How an answer happens

Pick up where you left off

Collect now lists what people asked of you in Slack. Continue in Claude Code, Codex or opencode opens a session that already has the thread, what Pace found and how related work ended last time.

Your Todo and the handoff

Pick up on your other Mac

With Pacenote on two Macs, your Todo and how each task went are the same on both, and Pace answers on the one you're using. Lock it, close the lid or step away, and Pace moves to the other one. Your Macs find each other on your network, with no server in between.

How your Macs hand over

Wrap up with a reply you send

When the work is done, the task closes with a note on how it ended. If someone is waiting, you get a drafted reply to copy into the thread. Pace never sends it for you.

How a task closes

Share one Slack app with your team

With a Pacenote hub, your team shares one Slack app, and each mention still goes to the Mac of the person who wrote it, answered with their own login. Pacenote writes the one command that installs the hub on a Linux server.

Teammates and the hub

What Pace never does

  • Answer anyone but you
  • Answer in private channels or DMs
  • Send messages for you
  • Change your files
  • Hand your credentials to the model
  • Open a public endpoint or phone home
  • Charge you
Why, and how to check each one

brew install --cask ashon/tap/pacenote
Setup steps

Needs an Apple silicon Mac on macOS 12 or later, Claude Code or Codex signed in with your plan, and a Slack workspace where you can create an app.
