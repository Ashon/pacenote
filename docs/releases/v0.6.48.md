## Scrolling

- A scrollbar shows while you scroll, or while you hold it, and fades away a moment after you stop. Pointing at a list
  no longer brings it up.
- Every scrollbar now lies over the content rather than taking a column of its own. That includes the small boxes that
  used the system's scrollbar: a session's command output and the text it asks you about, its plan, the list in
  **Group with**, and **Details** in setup. Text in them that could run wide now wraps.
- In those boxes, as at the window's corners, the scrollbar bends round a rounded corner rather than being cut off.

## Your phone and iPad

- The phone and iPad keep their own scrolling; nothing changes there.
