Settings > Tools fills in from your Mac: under the settings you would type, it offers what this Mac already has (your
work folder, GitHub orgs, Kubernetes contexts and SSH hosts) to pick with a click.

```sh
brew update && brew upgrade --cask pacenote
```

## Pick instead of typing

- Open a connection in Settings > Tools, and its settings show **On this Mac**: the names found here. Click one to put
  it in, and again to take it out. A name you pick is an unsaved change like one you type, so Save and Revert work the
  same.
- **Work folder**: the folders in your home with repositories in them, with how many each has.
- **GitHub**: your gh account and the orgs it belongs to. Without gh, the owners of the repositories in your work
  folder.
- **Kubernetes**: the contexts in your kubeconfig.
- **SSH hosts**: the users, keys and private address ranges of the hosts in your SSH config, its `Include` files too.
  Git hosting (GitHub, GitLab, Bitbucket) and VMs on this Mac are left out.
- Pacenote reads names and paths only: a key is found by its path and never opened. Your phone and iPad never get
  these names.
- When gh isn't signed in, or gh or kubectl is missing, the setting says what to do, with **Look again** for after.
