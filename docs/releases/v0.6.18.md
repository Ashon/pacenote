Manage your team hub from Pacenote: choose who can use it, add the people who ask to join, and see each member's Mac.
The hub's admins find it in Settings > Messengers.

```sh
brew update && brew upgrade --cask pacenote
```

## Team hub

- **Manage the hub**: under the hub's pairing in Settings > Messengers, its admins see who is on the hub and whether
  each member's Mac is connected now, or when it was last seen. Other members see nothing new. No message or answer
  shows there: the hub tells Pacenote only who is on it and their Macs.
- **Who can use it**: **Everyone** in the workspace, or **Members you add**. Switching to members you add keeps everyone
  paired at the time on the list, so nobody using the hub is cut off.
- **Asking to join**: on a hub for members you add, anyone else who mentions Pace is told that the admins were asked,
  and waits under **Asking to join**. **Add** lets them in and tells them so in Slack; if the pairing code they sent is
  still good, their Mac goes on to confirm it. **Decline** tells them nothing.
- **Add a member** finds someone in the workspace by name. A member's menu has **Make admin**, **Disconnect their Mac**
  and **Remove from the hub**.
- **Admins**: the member who installed the hub is its first admin; on a hub from before, the member who paired first.
  `HUB_ADMINS` in hub.env names admins Pacenote cannot change. `HUB_ALLOWED_USERS` now fills the list once, after which
  admins keep it.

## Your devices

- The app for iPhone, iPad and Android, still in development, can add a task to your Mac's Todo. It shows there as
  added on that device.

## Upgrading

- Update the hub to see **Manage the hub**: run its install command again with 0.6.18 in the URL and `--version`, or
  set `pacenote-hub:0.6.18` in its `compose.yaml` and run `docker compose pull && docker compose up -d`. A hub before
  0.6.18 keeps working as it did, and Pacenote shows nothing to manage until it is updated.
- A hub with `HUB_ALLOWED_USERS` keeps those members on its list, now in `access.json` in its data folder.
