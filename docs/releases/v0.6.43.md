A session in Pacenote can be Codex's too, a session can be closed or wrapped up into its task from its page, and a
group of tasks folds as one.

```sh
brew update && brew upgrade --cask pacenote
```

## Codex sessions in Pacenote

- **New session** and a task's **Continue here** pick Claude Code or Codex. The last one picked stays. Codex is offered
  once it is on this Mac (0.163 or later) and signed in.
- Pacenote runs `codex app-server` for the session with your own ChatGPT sign-in. Codex keeps the conversation in its
  own log, so `codex resume` picks it up anywhere after.
- It goes on on its page as a Claude Code session does: live progress, stop, images with a message, and its steps read
  the same way, a command as Shell and a patch as Edit.
- Its modes are Codex's: read only, ask outside the folder (the default) or full access, a change applying from its
  next turn. What it asks (a command, a file change, network or write access) comes with Codex's reason.
- A session the Codex app or a terminal runs goes on there.

## Close and Wrap up

- **Close**, beside a session's title, ends its run here, for one Pacenote runs or a Codex one. It moves to Ended
  today and opens again once it writes to its log, as when you type to it again. Its tool keeps the conversation.
- **Wrap up**, beside the title of a session that is an open task's work: your AI reads its last turns (and its
  branch's pull request) and writes what was done and what is left. Change it, check the things left to keep as tasks
  after it, and the task closes with it. The session closes too unless you keep it open.
- A Codex session that wrote to its log today no longer stays open all day: it is open until you close it.

## Task groups

- A group's arrow folds the group, its summary and its tasks together, and Todo keeps it folded. A group stays open
  while one of its tasks is open.

## Your phone and iPad

- Starting a Codex session, Close and Wrap up are on the Mac for now. The phone and iPad get them in a later build.
