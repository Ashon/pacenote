A new install opens on Todo: write your tasks with no account and no AI, and let your AI help when you want it. Your Mac
also gets ready for the tasks you write on your phone before it pairs.

```sh
brew update && brew upgrade --cask pacenote
```

## A new install opens on Todo

- No first run takes the window. Todo opens with a card on top: **New task** writes one of your own, **Set up AI** lets
  your AI find what is asked of you, **Connect** brings your Todo from another of your Macs, and **Add a device** pairs
  your iPhone or iPad. Once a task is in, the card folds to one line.
- **Set up AI** opens the same three steps as before (your AI, where your work is, a first look) in a sheet over Todo.
  After the first look, Todo says what it added, with **Look at the Maybes**.
- Without an AI, Todo keeps the tasks you write: **Collect now** is **Find tasks with AI**, and Recap counts your tasks
  and offers **Set up AI** in place of Write it up.
- **New task** takes when it is due, in your words.
- Pace stays optional: **Set up Pace** on the card, or in Settings > General.

## Your devices

- The app for iPhone, iPad and Android, still in development, can hand your Mac the tasks it wrote before it paired.
  The Mac adds only the ones new to it, with how you left them there (done, dismissed, in progress, waiting), puts each
  in the project its words point to, and never changes a task it has.
- Open such a task and it says where and when you wrote it: "Added on iPhone 45m ago, before it paired with your Mac."
- A device no longer makes or changes your Mac's tasks by sending its list: from a device, Todo takes only what you
  decided and the stages you set.

## Upgrading

- A Mac set up already looks as it did: no start card, and Todo and Pace as you left them.
- Update your Macs before your phone takes the handover: a Mac before 0.6.23 turns it down, and the phone keeps its
  tasks until the Mac is updated.
