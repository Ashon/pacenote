Text is sharper on a regular (non-Retina) display. Latin is back in the system font, SF Pro, beside Pretendard's Hangul,
and every screen sets its text from one set of roles.

```sh
brew update && brew upgrade --cask pacenote
```

## Sharper text

- **SF Pro and Pretendard.** Host Grotesk, set in v0.5.26, blurred at the 11 to 14px most screens are made of on a 1x
  display, where macOS does not hint fonts. SF Pro has shapes drawn for small sizes and sits beside Pretendard's Hangul
  without resizing either, so a line that mixes Korean and English reads evenly. The app is lighter too: it no longer
  bundles a Latin font.
- **macOS's own smoothing.** Text keeps the stem darkening macOS gives it, instead of the thinner antialiased strokes.
- **Captions hold their weight.** The 11px text of counts, pills and the status bar is medium everywhere, never regular.

## One set of type roles

- **Text takes a role, not a size:** number, title, heading, section, row, copy, body, meta and caption. Each carries
  its size, line height, tracking and weight, so a row title or a meta line looks the same on every screen. The sizes
  are the ones the screens were laid out on, so nothing moves.
- **Figures line up.** Overview's totals use tabular figures.

## Colors and surfaces

- **Darker greens where text sits on a selected row.** The primary green and the running and succeeded colors darken a
  step in light mode, so they read at 4.5:1 or more on a selected row too. Dark mode is unchanged.
- **Icons in the brand green.** Overview's and a collection's section icons, too faint in the chart green, take the
  primary green; the chart green stays on bars.
- **Fewer corners and shadows.** Small chips round at 6px and setup cards at 14px, like the rest, and menus, tooltips
  and dialogs share one shadow.

## Also

- **pacenote.dev** uses the same pair of typefaces, and loads no font of its own.
