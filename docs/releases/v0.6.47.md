## Scrolling

- Where a screen's scrollbar runs into a rounded corner of the surface, at the top or the bottom, its thumb bends round
  the corner, following the curve, rather than being cut off by it. It bends only part of the way, so at either end it
  still stands at the side, its tip following the curve.
- Scrollbars that meet no corner, beside a list or in a code block, stay straight.

## Your phone and iPad

- The phone and iPad keep their own scrolling; nothing changes there.
