A long session opens fast and stays smooth, a session runs once however fast you send, and actions keep one name each.

```sh
brew update && brew upgrade --cask pacenote
```

## Sessions

- A session's page reads only what was written to its log since you last looked. A 350 MB log took close to two
  seconds on every look while it worked, and now takes under a millisecond after the first; a log over 512 MB could not
  be opened at all, and now opens in about a second.
- Two messages sent one after another while a session picks up from its log start one run, not two.
- **Continue in a copy here** that fails to start ends the copy alone. The session it copies keeps running.
- A turn's time reads 14:03 today and 11/27 14:03 before; point at it for the seconds.
- A step of a tool Pacenote does not know shows what it says in words, not its JSON.
- The line under the message box is one sentence. Point at it for where the conversation is kept.
- Beside a session's page, the dot of one waiting for your turn is larger and ringed, apart from one working.

## One name for each action

- A task that looks done offers **Done**, as its row does (it said Close it).
- A session's log on a task folds with **Hide the log** (it said Close the log).
- A page or doc a session made on claude.ai says **Open on claude.ai**.
- Wrap up's sheet reads plainer: **Wrap up this task**, **Still to do: keep as tasks**.
