Diagrams and charts in answers are drawn as Pacenote's app looks: its colors, its typeface and its shapes.

```sh
brew update && brew upgrade --cask pacenote
```

The upgrade quits the running app first and reopens it, once. Config and run history are untouched.

## After you upgrade

- **With diagrams on**, Settings > Sandbox says "Update needed" for the diagram renderer's image. Run "Set up built-in
  sandbox" once: it builds only that image again, in a few minutes. Until then diagrams render as before.

## Diagrams

- Shapes are the app's cards (white, a hairline, rounded corners), groups its wells, and lines and secondary text its
  muted color, all in Pretendard, the app's typeface.
- Pace marks what a step means as the app does: the key step, where it starts, a warning, an error, or a step skipped or
  done. In mermaid that is `A[Label]:::warn`, in dot `[class="warn"]`, with `accent`, `primary`, `warn`, `error` and
  `muted`.
- Charts have the app's rounded bars in the logo's gradient, legends with dots, and series in its chart colors. Pie
  slices, gantt tasks and sequence diagrams follow the same look.
- A mermaid block can no longer switch to another theme. A dot or vega-lite block can still set its own colors.
- Gantt grid lines are no longer black, and pie slices no longer faded.
