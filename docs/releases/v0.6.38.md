Sessions shows the sessions on your other Macs too, and a session's page lists your sessions on its left, as Settings
lists its sections.

```sh
brew update && brew upgrade --cask pacenote
```

## Your other Macs' sessions

- Sessions shows each Mac linked to this one under its name, after **This Mac**: its open sessions by project, and in
  **Ended today** the ones it ended today. They are read on that Mac as you look.
- Open one to follow its conversation and what it does now, as they come. You type into it, answer what it asks and
  stop it on the Mac it is on, and its page says so.
- Your other Macs only read your sessions: none of them can send to a session here, answer it, stop it or link it to
  a task.
- A Mac shows its sessions while Sessions is on there (Settings > Todo > Sessions on this Mac). Both Macs need this
  version: one before it shows as needing an update. What a session says goes only to your own Macs and devices, over
  the link they already share.

## Beside a session's page

- A session's page and **New session** list your sessions on the left: this Mac's open ones by project, your other
  Macs' under their names, then the ones ended today, the one open marked. **Sessions** at its top goes back to the
  whole screen, and **+** starts a new one. A narrow window leaves the list out.

## On a phone or iPad

- The app's build 534 has the list beside a session's page on an iPad. A phone shows one screen at a time, as before.
- A phone or iPad shows the sessions of the Mac it is paired with. Your other Macs' sessions show on a Mac.
