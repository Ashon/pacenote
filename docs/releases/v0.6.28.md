Your Claude Code and Codex sessions in one place: a new Sessions screen lists the sessions open on this Mac, where
each runs, and which ones wait for you.

```sh
brew update && brew upgrade --cask pacenote
```

## Sessions on this Mac

- **Sessions**, below Todo in the sidebar, is off until you turn it on. Its card first says what it found (how many
  Claude Code sessions are open and where, how many Codex sessions wrote today) and what it reads.
- Sessions are grouped by your area in You whose repository they work in, else by repository. Each says where it runs
  (the Claude app, claude.ai, the terminal or editor app it started in, or the Codex app), whether it is **Working**,
  waiting for **Your turn**, or quiet, its branch, the task it is linked to, and what you typed last.
- The sidebar counts the sessions waiting for your turn.
- Open a session in place for what you typed lately, its folder, when it started, its log (read only when you ask) and
  the command that continues it in a terminal. **Open in Claude**, **Open on claude.ai**, **Open in Codex** or **Show
  in** your terminal app takes you to where it runs.
- **Ended today** has the Claude Code sessions that closed since midnight.
- Open sessions come from Claude Code's own list (`claude agents`) and Codex's logs from today; `codex exec` runs and
  subagents are left out. opencode sessions come later.
- What it reads stays on this Mac; your phone and other devices do not see your sessions.

## Changes

- The **Settings > Todo** switch that linked sessions to tasks is now named **Sessions on this Mac**, as it also turns
  on this screen. It keeps its value.
- A compacted session's summary no longer shows as something you typed.
