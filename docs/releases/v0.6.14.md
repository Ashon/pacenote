## Your devices, coming

The app for iPhone, iPad and Android is in development and not released yet. This Mac is ready for it:

- **A key of its own.** Add a Mac gives a device a key made for it, never the key your Macs share or your Slack app. It
  asks "Does iPhone show this number?" and says what goes: that key and your Todo.
- **Remove one you lost.** Once a device has paired, Settings > Macs lists it under **Your devices**. Remove it on any
  of your Macs, and none of them lets it in again.
- **What a device sees.** It asks your Mac for what Pacenote's own screens show, and the Mac answers that alone: never a
  secret, a run's prompt, or the settings that stay on the Mac.
- **Away from home.** Each Mac tells those it links to its Tailscale addresses.

## Narrow windows

- **Overview**: its figures and the briefing wrap instead of being cut off.
- **Pace**: its buttons go under the title, and a log line's message under its time.
- **You**: an area's eight weeks go under it.

## Upgrading

- Nothing to do. Your Macs stay paired as they are.
