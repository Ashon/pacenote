Keys for the things you do most, shown on the controls while you hold ⌘, and a task you hand a session shown as its
card instead of tags.

```sh
brew update && brew upgrade --cask pacenote
```

## Keys

- Hold ⌘ for a moment and each control with a key shows it. Another key, a click or letting go of ⌘ hides them, and
  they don't show while a menu or dialog is open. The keys shown are the ones that work on the screen you are on.
- ⌘1 to ⌘7 go to the sidebar's sections, ⌘, to Settings, ⌘[ and ⌘] back and forward, and ⌘B folds the sidebar.
- ⌘N adds a task and ⇧⌘N starts a new session from anywhere. ⌘K searches Runs.
- ⇧⌘[ and ⇧⌘] move between tabs in Todo, Sessions and Recap; ⌘← and ⌘→ move Recap a day, week or month.
- With a task open, ⌘↩ is Done (Keep as a task in Maybe) and ⌘⌫ Dismiss.
- The keys work with a Korean input source on too.

## A task in a session

- When a task opens a session, its first message shows as the task's card: what kind of work, the repository, the
  title (it opens the task), who asked where and when, the due date and the summary. What came before it is folded,
  and opens in Slack.
- A group shows its name, what it is about and a card for each of its tasks.
- What Pacenote asks of Claude Code or Codex, such as how to start and how to close the task, folds under its own
  line, as written.
- **New session** for a task shows the cards with **Your note** under them. Your note goes after Pacenote's message,
  so the tool gets the same message as before. **Edit as text** edits it all as text.

## Your words in a session

- What you type in a session, sent and queued too, is drawn as Markdown. HTML shows as you wrote it, and Copy copies
  what you wrote.
