Your phone or iPad now talks to the sessions your Mac runs: start one from a task, chat with it, and answer what it
asks, while Claude Code does the work on the Mac.

```sh
brew update && brew upgrade --cask pacenote
```

## Sessions from your phone or iPad

- The Pacenote app on a phone or iPad paired with this Mac has **Sessions**: the sessions open on the Mac, and each
  session's page, as the Mac shows them.
- On a session Pacenote runs, you send messages, answer what Claude Code asks before it acts (**Allow**, **Allow in
  this session**, **Deny**), change how it asks, stop it and end it. Claude Code runs on the Mac with your own sign-in
  there; the phone only shows the conversation and sends what you type.
- A code task's **Continue here** works there too: the task and what came before it are written in as the first
  message, in the task's folder on the Mac, and **Start** runs it on the Mac.
- **New session** on Sessions starts one in a folder you have worked in on the Mac: a device picks from those, and
  cannot browse the Mac's folders or start one outside your work.
- Opening a session in a terminal or an app, and copying its resume command, stay on the Mac.
- It needs the app's next build and a Mac on this version; with an older Mac, the app says to update it.

## Privacy

- A session's conversation goes to your own phone or iPad over the sealed link they paired with, and to nothing else:
  not your other Macs, not a server. The device keeps none of it.
- The Mac's log notes what a device changed (a session started, a message sent), not what it read.
