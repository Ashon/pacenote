The You screen is Areas now, and a session's Latest button is glass.

```sh
brew update && brew upgrade --cask pacenote
```

## Areas

- The screen that lists where you contribute is called **Areas**, with an icon of its own, in place of **You**. It is
  the same screen at the same place: your areas, how you contribute to each, what is open there and what you did lately.
- Todo's first look and Recap's area links name it the same way.

## Sessions

- **Latest**, the button that takes a session's page back to its end, is frosted glass over the conversation: what is
  behind it blurs, and its shadow lifts it off the page, in light and dark. Before, it had no shadow, and in dark mode
  hardly any fill.

## Your phone and iPad

- The phone and iPad show the new name and icon from their next build.
