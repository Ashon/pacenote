Tasks that are one piece of work become a group in Todo: your AI suggests one, you say yes, and it fills the group in
with what the work is, who waits for what and what comes first.

```sh
brew update && brew upgrade --cask pacenote
```

## Groups of tasks

- After it fills in a task or collects new ones, your AI looks where a project has three or more open tasks. When some
  of them are one piece of work, a card over the list asks: **Group them** or **Not one piece**. It never groups them
  by itself, and a set you said no to is not asked again.
- You can group by hand too: **Group with…** in a task's More, and **Move out of the group** for one in a group. A task
  is in one group at most, and each stays a task of its own.
- A group is one row with its tasks indented under it: its name, how many tasks, who waits, how far along and the
  earliest due. Opened, it shows what your AI filled it in with: a summary, who waits for what, what comes first and
  why, and what is left, with **Undo** and **Fill again**. It fills the group again when its tasks change.
- Your AI fills a group in from its tasks alone, with no tools, and what it says is checked against the tasks it names.
- **Continue here** on a group starts one session with all its open tasks, in order.
- **Done all** closes its open tasks together with one note of what you did, which your AI writes first for you to
  change. Draft a reply on each task for whoever waits.
- An AI tool asking for a task's context gets its group too.
- **Settings > Todo > Group similar tasks** turns the suggestions and fills off.

## Your other Macs, phone and iPad

- Groups go to your other Macs with the rest of Todo. A Mac before 0.6.41 shows the tasks as before.
- The phone and iPad show the groups and act on them with the app's next build.
