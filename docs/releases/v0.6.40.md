What a session puts on claude.ai is a card on its page, to open with one click, and Overview's tokens per day counts
the sessions Pacenote runs and its help on your tasks.

```sh
brew update && brew upgrade --cask pacenote
```

## What a session made

- A page, a design or a doc a Claude Code session puts on claude.ai is a card in its conversation, where it was put up
  last: what it is, its version, how many times the session updated it, and **Only you** while only you can open it.
- A Codex session's cards are the files in its folder that its answers link: an HTML page, an image, a PDF or Markdown.
- **Made here** under the session's title lists them all.
- A link opens in your browser, signed in as you. A file opens in its app on this Mac, or in Finder.
- A task's details have a **Made here** line for what its sessions made. When only you can open one, it says to share
  it on claude.ai before you send its link. A reply drafted for the task links what was made for it.
- Pacenote reads all this from the session's own log. It keeps only the link and never opens claude.ai itself.

## Tokens per day

- Overview's **Tokens per day** has two more parts: **Sessions**, the turns of the Claude Code sessions Pacenote runs,
  and **Task help**, filling in a task you add, Looks done and reply drafts.
- **By source** says how many turns ran and what a turn spent on average, and how many tasks were filled in, checked
  done and drafted.
- Counting starts with this version, so earlier days show none.

## On a phone or iPad

- A session's page on the phone or iPad shows the same cards with the app's next build, a file by its name.
