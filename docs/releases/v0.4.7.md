Pace keeps going when something goes wrong, and tells you when it can't. On this Mac it reads less, and what it posts
is checked for secrets first.

```sh
brew update && brew upgrade --cask pacenote
```

The upgrade quits the running app first and reopens it. The bot gets up to 15 seconds to finish its requests; the rest
stop and resume on the next start. Config and run history are untouched.

## Before you upgrade

- With your own Slack app, Pace now needs to know who may ask: your Slack member ID in Settings > Messengers > Slack,
  or anyone in the workspace (`MENTION_ALLOWED_USERS`, or `MENTION_ANYONE=on`). Until then it shows Setup needed with
  the way there. Through the team hub nothing changes.
- On this Mac, codex needs 0.138 or later, and Pace's runs no longer load your `~/.codex/config.toml`: Pacenote passes
  the model and reasoning effort from its own settings.

## Pace keeps going

- A stop leaves nothing behind. Requests in progress get 15 seconds; then their reasoner runs stop (the CLI and
  whatever it started, or the container), and they resume on the next start without counting as a retry.
- Mentions waiting their turn get 👀 at once, and a restart picks them up.
- An answer is never posted twice: once posting begins, a restart does not answer again. One cut off partway gets ⚠️
  and a word to the asker alone.
- A `bot.json` left behind by a crash or a power cut no longer shows Pace as running elsewhere.
- When Pace exits on its own, the app starts it again after 3 seconds, then 6, 12 and so on up to 5 minutes, where it
  used to give up after a few tries. The Pace screen counts down, with Start now and Stop.

## The app

- **Open Pacenote when you log in** (Settings > General): it opens in the menu bar without a window, and starts Pace if
  Start Pace when the app opens is on.
- A notification when Pace needs attention while the window is closed: it stopped, it needs setup, it lost Slack for 2
  minutes or more, or it cannot answer (a rejected sign-in, a usage limit, the sandbox's proxy). Clicking it opens the
  screen to look at, and the menu bar shows ⚠︎ while it lasts.
- When a newer Pacenote is out, the status bar says **Update**, and Settings > General has the brew command and the
  release notes. The app looks twice a day; `PACENOTE_DESKTOP_UPDATE_CHECK=off` turns that off.
- **Copy diagnostics** (Settings > General > Help) gathers versions, Pace's status, the settings without their secrets
  and the latest logs for a bug report.
- A window whose process ends loads again, and a screen that fails shows Reload instead of going blank.
- Pace's mark turns while Pace is connected, four times as fast while it answers; stopped, a gray stop sign stands in
  for it.

## Safer on this Mac

- Answers are checked before they are posted: the secrets Pacenote holds and the common token formats are taken out,
  and the run notes how many.
- claude reads only the reference directory, and codex runs under its own permission profile. Neither reads `.env`
  files, Pacenote's folders or the usual credential folders, and the CLIs no longer get Pacenote's tokens.
- `~/.pacenote` is kept to your account (700, and `.env` 600).
- Pace checks that the CLI is installed, signed in and new enough, at start and every 5 minutes, and says what to do in
  the status bar.
- People from another organization in a Slack Connect channel are told Pace answers only its own workspace.
- Settings > Messengers > Slack: **Create the Slack app** opens Slack with Pacenote's manifest filled in.
