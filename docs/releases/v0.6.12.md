Todo comes first: a new install starts with your tasks and no Slack app to make, and Pace is optional.

```sh
brew update && brew upgrade --cask pacenote
```

## Todo

- **In progress and waiting.** More > Mark in progress, or a session on a task in the last day, puts it first in Open.
  More > Waiting on someone else moves it to Waiting until a collection finds something new in its thread, or until
  **Back to open**.
- **Today.** Above the tabs: the ask waiting on you, the task to pick up, and the one waiting longest on someone else.
  Counted from the list, with no model.
- **New task (⌘N).** A task of your own, in the project its words point to, which you can change.

## A first run with no Slack app

- **A new install opens on three steps:** your AI, where your work is (this Mac, then Slack through your AI tool's own
  connector), and a first collection. Then Todo opens.
- **Pace is optional.** It stays off until you set it up, from the first run or Settings > General. Meanwhile it does
  not start by itself or ask to be set up, and Overview, Runs and Pace leave the sidebar.

## Upgrading

- Pace keeps answering as it did. With Todo on, Pacenote now opens on Todo and says so once; **Open on Overview
  instead** keeps Overview first.
- Your other Macs on 0.6.11 keep your Todo in step: they show a task in progress or waiting as open.
