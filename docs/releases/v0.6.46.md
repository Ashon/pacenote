Todo shows the mark of the tool a session opens in, opencode has a mark of its own, and Continue here carries
Pacenote's.

```sh
brew update && brew upgrade --cask pacenote
```

## Todo

- Where Todo opens a session, it shows that tool's mark in place of a terminal or window icon: Claude's for Claude Code,
  OpenAI's for Codex and opencode's for opencode. That is the **Continue in another app** menu, a task's working line,
  **Open**, the session buttons in a task's detail, the folder dialog and the session log.
- **Continue here**, on a task and on a group, shows Pacenote's own mark in one color: the same ring and dot as the
  logo, in the color of the button.

## opencode

- opencode has a mark now: its frame, with the block inside drawn lighter, as its own logo has it. Where opencode did
  the work, Pace's mark gets opencode's circle, as it has Claude's and OpenAI's.

## Your phone and iPad

- The phone and iPad show the marks from their next build.
