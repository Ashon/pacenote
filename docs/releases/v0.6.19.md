Pick Pacenote's color: green as before, or blue, purple, pink, red, orange or yellow, under Theme in Settings > General.

```sh
brew update && brew upgrade --cask pacenote
```

## Color

- **Color** in Settings > General turns buttons, selections, the window's faint tint, and Pace's mark and spinner to
  the color you pick. Statuses keep their own colors.
- Every color reads as well as green, in light and in dark: the text on a button, and colored text on a card or a
  selected row. Yellow buttons take dark text.
- Each Mac keeps its own color. The app icon, the menu bar and pacenote.dev stay green.
- In Overview's day bars, a day's failed runs sit apart from the rest by a thin gap, so they read in any color.

## Your devices

- The app for iPhone, iPad and Android, still in development, has the same Color in its General, kept on each device.
