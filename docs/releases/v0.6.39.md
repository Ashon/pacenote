Pacenote's window is gray now: the color you pick shows only where it points, on buttons, selections and Pace's mark.

```sh
brew update && brew upgrade --cask pacenote
```

## Color only where it points

- The window's background, sidebar, cards, borders, text and shadows are gray, in light and in dark, whichever color
  you pick in Settings > General. A whole window in the color's tint tired the eyes.
- Your color stays on buttons, the selected row and the one under the pointer, focus rings, highlighted cards, charts,
  and Pace's mark and spinner. Statuses keep their own colors.
- Light and dark keep their brightness, so text reads as before and cards stand apart from the window as before.
- Diagrams Pace posts to Slack keep their green lines, as pacenote.dev does.

## On a phone or iPad

- The app's build 536 has the same gray window.
