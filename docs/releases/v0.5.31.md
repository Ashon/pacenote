Set up a new Mac from the one already set up: its Slack app and who you are come over once you compare a 6-digit
number, and setup reads more easily.

```sh
brew update && brew upgrade --cask pacenote
```

## A new Mac, from your other one

- **Add a Mac.** On the Mac already set up, Settings > Macs > Add a Mac waits 10 minutes for the new one, showing
  itself on your network only meanwhile.
- **Connect to your other Mac.** The new Mac's setup has a third way to reach Slack, beside the team hub and your own
  Slack app. It finds the waiting Mac on your network, or takes its address (Tailscale).
- **Compare, then Allow.** Both Macs show the same 6-digit number, from a key they agree on there and then. Someone in
  between on the network would make the numbers differ. Allow on the Mac already set up sends, encrypted, your Slack
  app's tokens, who Pace answers and how Todo collects, and turns on following on both.
- **The rest stays this Mac's.** The AI, where Pace runs, folders and ops tools are chosen on the new Mac as usual, and
  each Mac signs in to its AI itself. "@Pace it's me" is skipped, and starting Pace moves it to the new Mac.

## Also

- **Setup's lines break where they read well.** No word left alone at the end of a line, titles and leads in even
  lines, the Slack step's three choices one under another, and long paths broken after their slashes.
