A session's page works as an AI chat does now: what you send while Claude Code works waits its turn, its plan sits
above the box, and the box says how full its context is.

```sh
brew update && brew upgrade --cask pacenote
```

## A session's chat

- Send while Claude Code is on a turn: your message shows **Queued** after it, and Claude Code reads it once the step
  it is on is done.
- While it works, its plan sits above the box: how far along it is, the step it is on, and each step when you open
  it. The steps that only keep the plan leave the conversation.
- The box says how full the model's context is, as in **Opus 5.5 · 46%** (the % alone on a phone). Claude Code
  summarizes what came before as it fills.
- Answers and what you typed have **Copy**, shown on hover or focus, or after a long press on a phone. A long message
  of yours folds, with **Show all**.
- The page follows an answer as it comes and stays put once you scroll up or open a step. A round button at its foot
  takes you back to the latest, with a dot while Claude Code works.
- The box floats over the conversation, which scrolls on under it, with no line between them.
- Esc stops the turn. The Enter that ends a word being put together (Korean, Japanese, Chinese) no longer sends it.
  What you write stays with its session while you look at another page, in memory only.
- When a turn is done and Pacenote's window is not in front, a notification says what Claude Code did. Click it to
  open the session.

## On a phone or iPad

- The app's build 533 has the same chat. On a phone, Return is a new line and **Send** sends. What waits its turn,
  the plan and the context gauge need a Mac on this version.
