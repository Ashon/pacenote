## Your AI fills in a task you add

- Add a task with **New task** or on your phone, or let a device hand over the ones it wrote before it paired. It is
  kept as you wrote it at once, and a moment later your AI fills in what it lacks: its project, what kind it is (code, a
  reply, other), two or three sentences of what to know, the open task it carries on from, who asked and when it is
  due. Who asked and when it is due come only from your words.
- Your AI is given your projects, your open tasks, how your tasks ended lately and, for the repositories the task's
  words point to, their state on this Mac: the branch, recent commits, commits not pushed and changes not committed. It
  runs with no tools, on the AI in Settings > Todo > Pacenote's own work, with your own login as Collect now does. It
  changes nothing else and sends nothing.
- What you set stays: the title, a project you picked and a due date you wrote.
- The row says **Claude Code is filling it in**, then **Filled in just now** for a day. Open it to see what was filled in
  and by which AI, with **Undo**, which takes it back on your other Macs too.
- A task that looks like one you have asks: **Same task** dismisses the new one and opens the other, and **Keep both**
  keeps both.
- Tasks you add one after another go together. When your AI is at its limit or signed out, the task stays as you wrote
  it.
- Turn it off in Settings > Todo > **Fill in new tasks**.

## Your devices

- The app for iPhone, iPad and Android, still in development, says that your Mac's AI fills in a task you add there,
  shows when your Mac is filling it in and what it filled in, and has **Undo**.
- With no Mac paired, the app's Recap counts the tasks on the device.

## Upgrading

- Filling in starts with the next task you add. The tasks you have are left as they are.
- A Mac before 0.6.24 shows a fill as an update, with no Undo or Same task. Update your Macs before your phone asks
  them for either.
