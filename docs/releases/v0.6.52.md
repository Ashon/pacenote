A session you leave rests instead of ending: it stays open, and its task opens it again, until you close it.

```sh
brew update && brew upgrade --cask pacenote
```

## Sessions

- A Claude Code session that is a task's, or that Pacenote ran, no longer moves to **Ended today** once nothing runs it
  (Pacenote lets its run go after 30 minutes, or its terminal or app closed). It stays open, your turn or quiet as it
  was, and typing on its page goes on from where it stopped.
- A task's session stays open past today, until you close it or close the task. Closing the task moves its sessions to
  Ended, a Codex one once it stops writing.
- **Close** is on a resting session's page too.

## After the update

- Sessions of your open tasks from the last two weeks that you never closed show in Sessions again. Close the ones you
  are done with from their page.
- A session Pacenote ran for no task goes back to Ended when Pacenote restarts.

## Your phone and iPad

- They show these sessions open from your Mac now. The note on a new session comes with the app's next build.
