Light and dark change as one: when the theme changes, the whole window fades to its new colors together, instead of some
parts going first and others following.

```sh
brew update && brew upgrade --cask pacenote
```

## Theme

- **One fade**: when you pick **System**, **Light** or **Dark** in Settings > General, or macOS switches between light
  and dark, the window fades to its new colors as one picture. Before, some parts took the new colors at once while
  buttons and rows eased into theirs.
- The window opens in its theme with no fade, and when it comes back in front it looks at macOS's light or dark again.
- With **Reduce motion** on in macOS, the fade is a short one.

## Inside

- The app's main process and its screens are in smaller parts, one for each area: the window, the menu bar item,
  Settings and Todo each keep their own code. Nothing changes in how they work.

## Upgrading

- Nothing to do.
