Settings > Macs is now Settings > Devices, and Add a Mac is Add a device: your iPhone, iPad and Android phone pair
there as well as your Macs.

```sh
brew update && brew upgrade --cask pacenote
```

## Devices

- **Settings > Devices** has your Macs and, once one has paired, your phones and tablets. **Your Macs** keeps its card
  there.
- **Add a device** is what Add a Mac was: a new Mac sets up from this one, a Mac set up already pairs with it, and a
  phone or tablet gets a key of its own. The app for iPhone, iPad and Android is still in development.
- **Phones and tablets** is what Your devices was: the devices paired with your Macs, and **Remove** for one you lost.
- A new Mac's setup and the messages about pairing say the new names too.

## Upgrading

- Nothing to do. Your Macs and devices stay paired as they are. A Mac still on 0.6.14 says Add a Mac where this one says
  Add a device.
