Your sessions and your tasks, linked: link a session to a task, or make a task of it, from Sessions, and see on Todo
which tasks have a session waiting for your turn.

```sh
brew update && brew upgrade --cask pacenote
```

## Sessions and tasks

- Open a session in **Sessions**: one not linked to a task can be **linked to a task** (your open tasks, its
  repository's first) or **made a task** of its own, in its repository's project. One that looks like an open task's
  work says which, to **Link**, or to say it is **Not it**.
- **Unlink** takes a session off its task: it is not found for that task again, on this Mac or your other Macs.
- On **Todo**, a task with a session open says how it stands on its row: **Working** or **Your turn**, and where it
  runs. Its button opens that session where it runs (**Open in Claude**, **Show in** your terminal app), rather than
  starting another one beside it; Continue moves under the row's menu.
- Opened, the task's session line has the same state and button.
- **Today** has a **Sessions** line: how many wait for your turn or work, how many more are quiet, and in which
  projects. It takes you to Sessions.
- All of it shows with **Sessions on this Mac** on (Settings > Todo).
