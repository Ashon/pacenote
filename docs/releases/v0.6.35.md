A session's page says where the session runs as soon as you move it here, and a session's page loads in its own
shape.

```sh
brew update && brew upgrade --cask pacenote
```

## Fixes

- Sending to a session resting in the Claude app or a terminal moves it here, but its page went on naming the app or
  the terminal, without what a session Pacenote runs has (**End it here** among them), until Sessions was read again.
  The page now reads the session again when it was moved here or let go, so it says where it runs at once. This
  fixes it on a phone or iPad paired with this Mac too.
- A session's page and **New session** load in their own shape, a few turns and the box at their foot, not as task
  rows.

## On a phone or iPad

- The app's build 532 tidies its screens on a phone: a row on Sessions opens its session, a session's box starts one
  line tall, a session Pacenote runs has **End it here** in the bar's **...**, and Recap's **Write it up** sits under
  Day, Week and Month, so they stay put. It works with a Mac on v0.6.34 too; the fix above needs this version.
