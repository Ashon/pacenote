A slimmer scrollbar, so it no longer looks heavy where it bends round a rounded corner.

```sh
brew update && brew upgrade --cask pacenote
```

## Scrolling

- The scrollbar is a little over half as wide as it was. It still bends round the rounded corners and fades a moment
  after you stop scrolling.
- The strip you grab it by keeps its width, so dragging it is as easy as before.

## Your phone and iPad

- The phone and iPad keep their own scrolling; nothing changes there.
