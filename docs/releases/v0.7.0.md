Pacenote 0.7 marks your sessions in Pacenote: the Claude Code and Codex sessions on your Mac in one place, each a page
where you go on with it, linked to the task it is for.

```sh
brew update && brew upgrade --cask pacenote
```

## From 0.6.52

The app is 0.6.52 with one line fixed: the first run's step on your projects says you fix them later on **Areas**, the
screen's name since 0.6.45. On pacenote.dev, the guide has a page for
[Sessions](https://pacenote.dev/guide/sessions/), the post
[Your sessions, in Pacenote](https://pacenote.dev/news/sessions-in-pacenote/) tells the whole of it, and the privacy
page now lists what goes to your AI when it fills in or groups your tasks, wraps one up or carries what you type to a
session, and your sessions shown on your other Macs.

## How the sessions came in

- 0.6.28: **Sessions** lists the Claude Code and Codex sessions on this Mac, where each runs and whether it waits for
  you.
- 0.6.29: a session you start yourself is linked to the task it worked on.
- 0.6.30 and 0.6.31: a session's page in Pacenote, where you type to it and see what it does now.
- 0.6.32: **Continue here** starts a session in Pacenote, with the other tools in its menu.
- 0.6.34 to 0.6.36: one conversation goes on where you type last, and its page works as an AI chat does.
- 0.6.38: your other Macs' sessions, and your sessions beside a session's page.
- 0.6.40 and 0.6.42: what a session made on claude.ai, and images with a message.
- 0.6.43: Codex sessions in Pacenote, **Close** and **Wrap up**.
- 0.6.50: a task handed to a session shows as its card, and Markdown in what you send.
- 0.6.52: a session you leave rests, open until you close it.
