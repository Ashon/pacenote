Pace answers only you. It runs on your Mac with your own Claude or ChatGPT login, so it is your assistant, not one for
the whole channel: anyone else who mentions it is told so, and only they see it.

```sh
brew update && brew upgrade --cask pacenote
```

The upgrade quits the running app first and reopens it, once. If Pace already answered only you, nothing changes.

## Who Pace answers

- **One person: you.** With your own Slack app, Pace takes one Slack member ID, yours, which setup fills in when you
  say `@Pace it's me`. With an empty or longer list Pace does not start, and Settings will not save more than one.
- **"Anyone in the workspace" is gone.** The app removes the setting as it starts. If you used it, or listed other
  people, Pace waits for you to say who you are: Overview leads you to setup or Settings.
- **Teammates run their own Pace** through your team's hub, each with their own login. A hub desktop now checks every
  mention itself and answers only the member it is paired as, whatever the hub sends it.
- **Anyone else** who mentions Pace gets "I only answer the person I work for.", which only they see. A request someone
  else left waiting is not taken up after a restart.
- **Ops tools** turn on with the team hub too, since Pace answers only you there as well.
