A message to a Claude Code session in Pacenote can carry images: paste a screenshot, drop a file or pick some, and
they go with what you typed.

```sh
brew update && brew upgrade --cask pacenote
```

## Images with a message

- In a session's message box, paste an image, drop one on it or pick some with its image button. PNG, JPEG, GIF or
  WebP, up to 10 a message. Each shows over the box, with a button to take it out.
- Each goes at most 1568 pixels on its longest side and 1 MB, as much as Claude looks at. A larger one is made smaller
  on the way. Another kind of file is left out, and the box says why.
- They go to Claude Code as images pasted into it do, and stay in its own log. Pacenote keeps none of them.
- Your turn shows them, and one opens larger.
- While a session asks you something, your answer goes without images.

## While it answers

- A session's page stays at the end while more comes in, images loading one after another included. Only a scroll up
  by your hand takes it away.

## Your other Macs, phone and iPad

- They show how many images a turn had, the phone and iPad with the app's next build. A message sent from the phone
  or iPad goes without images.
